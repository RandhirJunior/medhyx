    Senior Software Engineer – Ab Initio & AWS

    Join a Medhyx engineering team at a leading global bank’s technology risk and controls office. Design, build and run Ab Initio data applications on AWS, from containerisation and CI/CD to APIs, releases and production support.

    Ab Initio · Business Rules Engine · AWS · Docker · Kubernetes · CI/CD · APIs

    Full job description

    About the role

    You will work within the technology controls office of a leading global bank, a team of risk and engineering specialists who help data and analytics teams build a secure, risk-aware organisation. The team designs intelligent controls for identity, protection, detection, response, recovery and resilience, delivered through practical risk frameworks, automated controls and simple processes.

    Alongside hands-on engineering, you will help execute strategic initiatives that lift team capability and performance, embed a proactive risk culture, and advise senior stakeholders on emerging risks and risk maturity in financial services.

    Reports to: Engineering Manager

    What you’ll do

    • Ab Initio development & engineering: design, develop, enhance and maintain Ab Initio applications and data processing solutions; troubleshoot complex issues, optimise performance and provide production support.
    • CI/CD & AWS deployment: own end-to-end CI/CD for AWS, including build, release, deployment, configuration and validation across development, test, staging and production.
    • Containers & Kubernetes: containerise Ab Initio applications with Docker and deploy and manage images in Kubernetes clusters on AWS.
    • API development & integration: design, build, test and deploy APIs, promote them through environments and validate integrations.
    • Maintenance, BRE & releases: fix defects, update Business Rules Engine (BRE) logic, make code changes and ship releases through established pipelines.
    • End-to-end delivery ownership: requirements, development, source control, containerisation, CI/CD, cloud deployment, testing, release management and production support.

    Technical skills

    Ab Initio · Business Rules Engine (BRE) · Amazon Web Services · Docker · Kubernetes · CI/CD pipelines · API development & integration · cloud deployment · release & environment management · production support & incident resolution · testing, validation & troubleshooting

    What you bring

    • A strong risk mindset: you proactively identify, discuss and act on current and emerging risks.
    • Analytical and critical thinking, using data-driven insight to assess risks and recommend actions.
    • Clear written and verbal communication, and the ability to build relationships with stakeholders at all levels.
    • Experience designing controls; knowledge of operational and compliance risk frameworks is an advantage.
    • People leadership skills, including facilitating, coaching and guiding others.
